# Mascote do Helpy — pedido para o gerador de imagem (ChatGPT / gpt-image)

Cole o bloco "PEDIDO" inteiro na sessão que gera imagens. Ele pede 3 conceitos;
depois de escolher um, cole o bloco "CAMADAS" com o conceito escolhido anexado.

## Quem é o personagem (para entender, não precisa colar)

O Helpy é atendimento que nunca fecha: a "senha" do balcão (Nº 0042) virou
conversa. A marca é um **balão de conversa com os picotes de uma senha de fila**
(meia-lua recortada no meio de cada lado) e uma pontinha de balão embaixo à
esquerda, com **dois olhinhos em pílula**. O mascote novo é essa marca virando
um personagem 3D — nada de robô, viseira, fone ou astronauta.

## PEDIDO (3 conceitos)

Generate 3 separate square images (1024×1024), each one a different pose of the same character. Transparent background (PNG with alpha). No text, no letters, no numbers, no logos, no watermark.

Character: the mascot of "Helpy", a customer-service brand. Its ENTIRE body is one inflated speech bubble shaped like a queue ticket:
- a soft rounded rectangle, a little wider than tall, with generous rounded corners;
- one semicircular notch cut into the middle of the LEFT side and one into the middle of the RIGHT side, like a perforated ticket stub;
- a small curved speech-bubble tail at the bottom-left;
- the front face is perfectly smooth and BLANK — absolutely NO eyes, NO mouth, NO facial features (the eyes will be added later in code);
- two short, stubby, rounded arms in white, attached low on the sides (no fingers, mitten-like rounded hands).

Material and render: premium designer vinyl toy, soft-touch matte with a faint satin sheen and gentle subsurface scattering, slightly squishy and inflated. Body color cobalt blue #1F66F4, a touch lighter on the top surface (#3D7BF6), deeper in the shadowed underside (#1550C8). Arms and accents pure white. Soft studio key light from the upper-left, subtle cool rim light, soft ambient occlusion where arms meet the body. Clean, minimal, friendly, high-end — think Apple product render meets Pixar. Not childish, not glossy plastic, no chrome, no metal, no robot parts, no visor, no headphones, no antennas.

Poses:
1. Three-quarter view, floating and slightly tilted, LEFT arm raised waving hello, right arm resting.
2. Front view, both arms opening in a welcoming gesture, a tiny bounce.
3. Three-quarter view from slightly below, one arm holding up a small blank white ticket stub (with the same side notches) like presenting a number.
